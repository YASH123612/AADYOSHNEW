Delete Your Account

Ask us to permanently remove your account and personal data.

This is a starter policy written to match how this site works today. Replace the bracketed details with your real business information and have a qualified legal professional review it before you rely on it.

Last updated: 18 September 2026

1. Important

Account deletion is permanent. Once it is complete, we cannot restore your access or recover your data, except where we are required to keep certain records by law. Download anything you want to keep — certificates, project files, notes — before you request deletion.

2. How to delete your account

aadyosh accounts are created and signed in through our authentication provider, Clerk. We do not yet offer a self-service "delete account" button inside your account settings, so please use the form below:

  • 1. Request: submit the form below from the email address on your account.
  • 2. Verify: we confirm it is really you, usually by replying to that email.
  • 3. Delete: we complete the deletion within 30 days and email you when it is done.

3. What gets deleted

  • Your profile, sign-in, and session data held with Clerk.
  • Your learning progress, enrolments, and any project submissions tied to your account.
  • Your entries in our roadmap, support, Campus Captain, and deletion-request records, once the deletion is processed.
  • Access to program content, recordings, and certificates tied to the account.

4. What we may retain

Some information may be kept for a limited time after deletion, only where we have a legal or security reason to:

  • Payment and invoicing records, where required for tax or accounting purposes.
  • Records needed to show that we acted on your deletion request.
  • Security and fraud-prevention logs.

Retained data is limited to what is necessary and is typically kept for no longer than [12] months, unless the law requires a longer period.

5. Need help?

If you have trouble with the form below, or want to check on a request you already submitted, email hello@aadyosh.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" from the email address on your account, or use the Support page.

6. Request account deletion

Request received

We'll email you to verify it's you, then complete the deletion. Questions? Write to hello@aadyosh.com.
