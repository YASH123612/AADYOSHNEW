Pricing & Refund Policy

How our pricing works, when you can get a refund, and how duplicate payments are handled.

This is a starter policy written to match how this site works today. Replace the bracketed details with your real business information and have a qualified legal professional review it before you rely on it.

Last updated: 18 September 2026

1. Scope

This policy explains how aadyosh ("we", "us") handles pricing, cancellations, refunds, and duplicate payments for programs purchased on this website. When you pay for a program, you also agree to our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.

2. Transparent pricing

The fee for each program is shown before you pay, along with what it includes. Where a price is confirmed on a screening call, the amount agreed on that call is the amount you pay. Discounts and launch offers are described at the time they apply and may not be available afterwards.

3. Refund window

  • You may request a full refund within 7 days of enrolment, provided you have not completed more than one live session and have not downloaded program materials.
  • After this window, fees are non-refundable, except in the situations described in section 4.
  • Requesting to switch to a different program or a later cohort is not the same as a refund — see our Program Change & Rescheduling Policy.

4. When we refund in full

  • We cancel a cohort and cannot offer you a seat in a comparable one within a reasonable time.
  • You were charged twice for the same enrolment, or charged an amount that does not match the price you agreed to (see section 7, duplicate payments).

5. Why we restrict refunds after the window

Our cohorts run with limited seats and mentor capacity that we commit to you as soon as you enrol:

  • Limited seats: each batch has a fixed number of spots, and a late withdrawal takes a seat that could have gone to someone else.
  • Mentor and reviewer time: mentor slots, project review bandwidth, and evaluation capacity are reserved per learner ahead of the batch.
  • Access starts immediately: you can typically access materials, the community, and scheduling as soon as you enrol, before the batch itself begins.

6. When refunds are not available

  • You stop attending, or lose interest, after the refund window in section 3 has passed.
  • Your account is suspended or closed for breaching our Terms & Conditions.
  • Fees paid for services that have already been delivered, such as a completed mock interview, resume review, or 1:1 mentor session.

7. Duplicate or incorrect payments

If you are charged twice for the same enrolment, or charged an amount that does not match what you agreed to, we will refund the difference once we have verified it. Email hello@aadyosh.com with both payment references (or screenshots), your registered email or phone number, and the program name.

8. Changing your cohort or program

If you cannot join your assigned batch, you can ask to move to a later cohort of the same program, or switch to a different program, before your batch's first live session. This is handled under our Program Change & Rescheduling Policy, not as a refund, and depends on seat availability in the new batch.

9. How to request a refund

Email hello@aadyosh.com from your registered email address with your full name, program name, and payment reference, or raise a ticket on the Support page. Include:

  • Full name and the email or phone number you enrolled with.
  • Program name and batch.
  • Payment reference or transaction ID.
  • Reason for the request (optional, but it helps us process it faster).

We aim to review and respond to requests within 3–5 working days.

10. How approved refunds are paid

Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method within 7–10 working days of approval, where the payment provider allows it; otherwise we will agree an alternative method with you. Your bank or card network's own processing times may add a few extra days on top of that.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows when it last changed. Continuing to use aadyosh after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

Questions about pricing or refunds? Write to hello@aadyosh.com or use the Support page.
