Pricing & Refund Policy

How our pricing works, why payments are non-refundable, and how duplicate payments are handled.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

1. Scope

This policy explains how aadyosh ("we", "us") handles pricing, cancellations, refunds, and duplicate payments for programs purchased on this website. When you pay for a program, you also agree to our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.

2. Transparent pricing

The fee for each program is shown before you pay, along with what it includes. Where a price is confirmed on a screening call, the amount agreed on that call is the amount you pay. Discounts and launch offers are described at the time they apply and may not be available afterwards.

3. Payments are non-refundable

Once a payment has been successfully processed, it is non-refundable. This applies whether you attend every session or none, and whether you cancel before or after your batch starts.

Please read this before you pay. Ask your questions on the screening call first, because we cannot reverse a completed payment.

The only exceptions are the ones listed in section 4 below, or a cancellation window that is stated on the checkout page of a specific program. If a program offers such a window, the checkout page is what applies to that program.

  • Moving to a later batch or a different program is not a refund — see our Program Change & Rescheduling Policy.
  • Any scholarship, discount, or fee-benefit that a program describes is separate from a cancellation refund and follows the conditions stated with that offer.

4. The exceptions

  • Duplicate or incorrect charge: you were charged twice for the same enrolment, or charged an amount that does not match the price you agreed to. We refund the difference after verifying it (see section 7).
  • Cohort cancelled by us: we cancel your batch and cannot offer you a seat in a comparable one within a reasonable time.
  • A program page says otherwise: the checkout page of that program states a cancellation window, and your request falls inside it.

5. Why refunds are restricted

Our programs run in cohorts, and most of what we spend on you is committed the moment you enrol:

  • Limited seats: each batch has a fixed number of places, and a withdrawal part-way through cannot be filled by someone else.
  • Reserved time: mentor sessions, project review capacity, and evaluation effort are set aside for you before the batch begins.
  • Early costs: payment processing, onboarding, and support start straight away.
  • Access starts early: you can usually reach materials, the community, and scheduling soon after you enrol, before the batch itself starts.

6. Situations that are not refunded

  • You stop attending, change your mind, or can no longer commit the time.
  • Your account is suspended or closed for breaching our Terms & Conditions.
  • You did not receive a job or interview call. We do not guarantee those outcomes (see our Terms & Conditions).
  • Services already delivered, such as a completed mock interview, resume review, or 1:1 mentor session.

7. Duplicate or incorrect payments

If you are charged twice for the same order, or charged an amount you did not agree to, we will refund the extra amount once we have verified it. Email hr@aadyosh.com with both payment references (or screenshots), your registered email or phone number, and the program name.

8. Changing your cohort or program

If you cannot join your assigned batch, you can ask to move to a later cohort of the same program, or switch to a different program, before your batch's first live session. This is handled under our Program Change & Rescheduling Policy, not as a refund, and depends on seat availability in the new batch.

9. How to make a request

For a duplicate payment, a cancelled cohort, or any refund question, email hr@aadyosh.com from your registered email address, or raise a ticket on the Support page. Include:

  • Your full name and the email or phone number you enrolled with.
  • Program name and batch.
  • Payment reference or transaction ID.
  • What happened (a screenshot of a duplicate charge helps).

We aim to review and respond within 3–5 working days.

10. How approved refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method where the payment provider allows it; otherwise we will agree another method with you. How long it takes to reach you depends on the payment gateway and your bank or card network, so it can take several working days after we approve it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows when it last changed. Continuing to use aadyosh after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

Questions about pricing or refunds? Write to hr@aadyosh.com or use the Support page.
